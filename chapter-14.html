Reading the Vision 20 Label

What the published label records say, what can vary, and how to verify the bottle before purchase.

Published label details

Publicly available label records for Vision 20 by Zenith Labs describe a one-capsule serving and 30 capsules per bottle. A label record entered in 2025 lists zinc 50 mg, copper 1 mg, bilberry fruit extract 160 mg, ginkgo leaf extract 160 mg, maritime pine bark extract 150 mg, lycopene 20 mg, astaxanthin powder 12 mg, and LuteMax 2020 as lutein 20 mg and zeaxanthin 4 mg. An earlier label record presents LuteMax 2020 differently. That is why the current physical packaging should be treated as the controlling source.

The product’s public marketing materials describe one capsule daily. Follow the package directions and the advice of your healthcare professional; do not use an old web transcription if the bottle in front of you says something different.

Check more than the ingredient names

Read serving size, amount per serving, percent Daily Value, inactive ingredients, manufacturer or distributor information, warnings, directions, and lot or expiration information. Note whether a label names a specific extract or trademark ingredient. If a number or name is unclear, contact the manufacturer or ask a pharmacist to help interpret it.

Resolve conflicting records

Online label databases may preserve older versions, and websites may summarize the same ingredient in different ways. For Vision 20, the different public presentations of LuteMax 2020 show why a shopper should not assume that every web page refers to the same label revision.

Before ordering, verify what the current package contains. After receiving it, compare the bottle with the information used to make the decision. A transparent product review should make these uncertainties visible instead of hiding them.
