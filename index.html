THE PRODUCT AT A GLANCE

What is Vision 20?

Vision 20 is a dietary supplement marketed by Zenith Labs. Its public label records describe a one-capsule serving with minerals, carotenoids, and botanical extracts. The manufacturer positions it for nutritional vision support; that positioning is not proof of disease treatment or restored eyesight.

Important safety detail: public label records list 50 mg of zinc per capsule. The NIH adult upper intake level is 40 mg daily from all sources, so this amount deserves a professional review before use.

How this review approaches health claims

Research about an ingredient does not establish that the finished Vision 20 formula delivers the same result. The guide avoids promises about reversing eye disease, restoring vision, or replacing professional care. It points readers toward the National Eye Institute, NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, NCCIH, and FDA for independent information.
